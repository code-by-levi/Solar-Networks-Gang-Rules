Solar Network — Gang Rules & Regulations

These rules apply to all registered gangs, leaders, members, prospects, and associates. Gang leaders are responsible for ensuring their members understand and follow these regulations. Failure to comply may result in warnings, strikes, suspensions, or forced disbandment.

1.0 Gang Code of Conduct

1.1 Dress Code — Gang Representation

  • Minimum of three identifiable uniform pieces must be worn during gang-related RP.
  • Uniforms must match gang colors and identity.
  • Wearing gang attire outside of gang RP is discouraged.

1.2 Vehicle Guidelines — Gang Representation

  • All members must use the same vehicle color scheme.
  • Vehicles should match gang identity.
  • Changing colors mid-operation to avoid identification is prohibited.
  • Police, government, or emergency-style vehicles require staff approval.

1.3 Toxicity & Conduct

  • Talking over bodies is allowed.
  • Toxicity must be kept to a minimum and remain within city/community rules.
  • Excessive harassment, hate speech, real-life threats, or targeted abuse are strictly prohibited.
  • Staff may intervene if behavior is deemed excessive or disruptive.

1.4 Org Rebranding — Identity Changes

  • Any changes to a gang’s name, colors, theme, or branding must be approved.
  • Submit an Org Management Ticket before making any changes.
  • Rebranding without approval may result in disciplinary action or disbandment.

2.0 Gang Hostilities & Conflict Rules

2.1 War Declaration Requirements

  • All war declarations must be approved by Org Staff.
  • Wars must be submitted through the DEC Channel using the official template (see pinned messages).
  • No war is valid without staff approval.
  • Spawn camping is prohibited.
  • Holding compounds or territory without proper RP escalation is not allowed.

2.2 Hand Holding — Unrealistic Cooperation

  • Hand holding of any kind is prohibited.
  • Gangs may not jointly defend compounds, or operate as a single organization agaisnt police without prior approval from gang management
  • External gangs must not interfere in active hostilities unless they have an approved active declaration against the same organization.
  • Temporary cooperation must have clear RP reasoning and may require staff approval.

2.3 Escalation Rules — Conflict Progression

  • Hostilities must build naturally through roleplay.
  • Meetings or verbal confrontations.
  • Melee fights, kidnappings, or interrogations.
  • Official War Declarations after RP buildup.

Escalation should reflect a naturally developing conflict. Organizations are expected to allow hostilities to build through continued roleplay before initiating KOS, which may ultimately progress into an official war declaration.

2.4 Hostile Event Limits — City Engagement Cap

  • Any hostile events in the city must remain within a capped limit to ensure fairness.
  • Large-scale or excessive hostile activity is not permitted unless explicitly authorized by Gang Staff.

2.5 Spray Zones — Hostile RP Areas

  • Spray zones are not KOS zones.
  • Spray zones are purely drug selling turf only, and does not effect the activitys of any player in or around that area
  • Normal server rules remain in effect, and spray zones do not permit random deathmatching or unrestricted KOS.

Spray zones are intended to create immediate hostile roleplay opportunities. While players may engage and rob participants without prior verbal interaction, these zones do not grant unrestricted KOS rights and all actions must remain reasonable, proportional, and directly related to the active spray.

2.6 Hostile Cooldowns, Robbery & Hostage Restrictions

  • Players who have left a gang or crew are subject to a 2-day hostile cooldown.
  • During this period, they are not permitted to participate in, assist, or support any hostile actions involving another gang or crew.
  • This includes direct involvement, planning, communication, or any form of external support during active conflicts.
  • Exceptions may only be granted with explicit approval from Gang Staff.
  • When robbing players during hostile or criminal RP, only illegal items directly related to criminal activity may be taken (e.g. drugs, weapons, marked illegal goods, black-market items).
  • Cash, bank money, or any form of personal financial compensation cannot be demanded or taken during robberies.
  • Players may not be taken hostage for the purpose of demanding money or financial gain.
  • Hostage RP must be used for situational leverage, information gathering, negotiation, or story-driven escalation only.
  • The only exception to this rule is if terms are formally set under an approved gang war agreement or staff-sanctioned war conditions.

These rules are designed to prevent unrealistic hostage scenarios, limit exploitative robberies, and ensure roleplay remains balanced, fair, and progression-based.

3.0 Gang Structure & Limits

3.1 Member & Prospect Requirements

Prospects are additional and do not count toward full member totals.

Syndicate Minimum 25 Members Hostile Cap 40

3.2 Structure Guidelines

  • Organizations must maintain their minimum member requirement to retain their tier.
  • Falling below minimum requirements for an extended period may result in a status review.
  • Prospects are considered trial members and should not hold leadership or decision-making roles.
  • Prospects are not permitted to participate in gang warfare or organized hostile actions.
  • If a hostile situation begins between organizations (Crew, Organization, or Syndicate), prospects must immediately disengage and leave the area.

3.3 Gang Responsibility & Integrity

  • Organizations are responsible for the actions of their members.
  • Cheating, exploiting, third-party software, glitches, or knowingly benefiting from cheats may result in strikes or disbandment.
  • Consequences may include warnings/strikes, financial penalties, hostility cooldowns, suspension, or forced disbandment.

4.0 Enforcement & Final Notes

  • Individual behavior reflects on the entire organization.
  • Repeated violations will result in escalating penalties.
  • Staff decisions are final.
  • When unsure, ask staff before acting.
  • “I didn’t know” is not a valid excuse.

🎯 Purpose of Gang RP

Gang roleplay should be immersive, balanced, fair, and story-driven. Quality RP creates the best experience for the entire Solar Network community.
